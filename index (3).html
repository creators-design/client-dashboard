October: the headline

  • Biggest highlight — Dr. Tarang Krishna had his strongest month on record: +56,679 Instagram followers (7.4% growth), 31.0M views, and the YouTube channel passed 100,000 subscribers. Cancer-screening Reels averaged 1.0M views each and produced 210,002 shares.
  • What didn't work — Volume without packaging. 28 of 83 Reels reached under 5,000 people, and the same clips were posted two or three times, with repeats reaching 1–3% of the original. Growth was front-loaded: week 1 added 25,228 followers, the final week only 6,649.
  • The ask — An introduction to one brand or founder in health, fintech or D2C who is ready to invest ₹5L+ a month. One confirmed client is the single trigger for the next hire, and warm introductions close far faster than outbound.
